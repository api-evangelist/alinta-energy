# Alinta Energy

> Alinta Energy is one of Australia's largest integrated energy retailers and generators (roughly 3,000 MW across gas, coal, wind, solar and battery; more than a million retail customers; owned by Chow Tai Fook Enterprises), and a designated, live Consumer Data Right (CDR) energy data holder. Its API posture is mandate-implemented but developer-closed: four operations are anonymously callable and everything of substance sits behind ACCC accreditation and consumer consent. There is no developer portal, no self-serve API, no proprietary OpenAPI, no SDK and no open grid or market data.

## What you can actually call anonymously

- Alinta-hosted CDR discovery: `GET https://public.cdr.alintaenergy.com.au/cds-au/v1/discovery/status` (header `x-v: 1`) — confirmed HTTP 200, 2026-07-27
- Alinta-hosted CDR outages: `GET https://public.cdr.alintaenergy.com.au/cds-au/v1/discovery/outages` (header `x-v: 1`) — confirmed HTTP 200
- AER-hosted plan catalogue: `GET https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/alinta/cds-au/v1/energy/plans` (header `x-v: 1`) — confirmed HTTP 200, 493 Alinta plans
- AER-hosted plan detail: `GET https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/alinta/cds-au/v1/energy/plans/{planId}` (header `x-v: 3`)

The plan catalogue is served by the Australian Energy Regulator's Energy Made Easy CDR platform on Alinta's behalf — Alinta's own host returns 404 for that path. This is the CDR energy architecture, not an Alinta defect.

## APIs

- [Alinta Energy CDR Discovery API](https://consumerdatastandardsaustralia.github.io/standards/#discovery-apis): Status + outages. Base `https://public.cdr.alintaenergy.com.au/cds-au/v1`
- [Alinta Energy CDR Generic Plan Data API](https://www.energymadeeasy.gov.au/): 493 electricity and gas plans. Base `https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/alinta/cds-au/v1`
- [Alinta Energy CDR Energy API](https://www.alintaenergy.com.au/help-and-support/terms-and-conditions/consumer-data-right-cdr): Accounts, balances, invoices, billing, payment schedules, concessions, service points, usage, DER. Accredited data recipients only; base URI not published.

## Notes for agents

- Send `x-v` on every call; it is mandatory and unversioned requests fail with 400/406.
- Errors are CDR error lists (`urn:au-cds:error:*`), not RFC 9457 problem+json.
- No idempotency contract, no webhooks, no AsyncAPI, no sandbox, no CLI, no SDK, no MCP server.
- Alinta's corporate site (`www.alintaenergy.com.au`) returns HTTP 403 to all automated clients; a 403 there is a WAF, not evidence of a resource.
